Services That Make Learning Easier

01

Online Catalogue

Explore our complete collection of books and find your favorite titles through our online catalogue.

Available: 24 Hours Online

How to Use

  1. Visit the library website.
  2. Open the Collections page.
  3. Search by title or author.
  4. Check book availability.
📖
02

E-books

Access digital books and educational resources from anywhere using your internet-connected device.

Available: 24 Hours Online

How to Use

  1. Visit the digital library section.
  2. Browse available e-books.
  3. Select your preferred book.
  4. Open or download the available resource.
🏛️
03

Study Room Booking

Reserve a comfortable and quiet study space for individual learning or group study sessions.

Timings: Monday - Saturday, 9 AM - 6 PM

How to Use

  1. Visit the library reception.
  2. Check available study rooms.
  3. Choose your preferred time slot.
  4. Confirm your booking.
🖨️
04

Printing & Scanning

Print your assignments, documents and study materials or scan important papers for digital use.

Timings: Monday - Saturday, 9 AM - 5 PM

How to Use

  1. Visit the library service desk.
  2. Provide your document.
  3. Select printing or scanning options.
  4. Collect your completed document.
📶
05

Free Wi-Fi

Stay connected with complimentary internet access for research, online learning and educational activities.

Available: During Library Hours

How to Use

  1. Bring your Wi-Fi enabled device.
  2. Open your device's Wi-Fi settings.
  3. Select the library Wi-Fi network.
  4. Enter the password provided by staff.

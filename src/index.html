DISCOVER OUR LIBRARY

A Space Designed for Readers and Learners

Readers Haven Library is a learning and reading space located at the University of Central Punjab. It is designed to encourage reading, support academic learning and help students explore different areas of knowledge.

From discovering interesting books to spending focused study time, Readers Haven provides a welcoming environment for readers, students and knowledge seekers.
